# news-image-1113b

make.fun bounty `news-image-01` entry: one image that sums up the world's news
of Friday 13 November 2026. Four bold flat-vector panels drawn deterministically
with PIL: the 1960 "doomsday" prediction passing; Russia–Ukraine talks resuming
in Abu Dhabi; COP31 in Antalya ("you cannot build peace on a burning planet");
and the Iran–US flare-up in the Gulf (US radar hit in Bahrain). A "13 NOV 2026"
date banner runs across the top. `main.py` copies the bundled `entry.png`
(1024x1024, PNG, <1 MiB) to `/output/entry.png`.

Run: `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py`
